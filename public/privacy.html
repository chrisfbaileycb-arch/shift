Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Local scans

Local scans are analyzed entirely in your browser. That content is not uploaded or stored.

AI Deep Scans

When you run an AI Deep Scan, the app name, configuration text, and marketing text you submit are sent to our backend and processed by an AI service to generate your report. The resulting report (scores and findings) is stored in your account history so you can review it later. Avoid submitting real credentials or secrets; the scanner only needs structure, not live keys.

Account data

If you sign in, we store your account identifier, your scan-credit balance, and your report history. Sign-in is handled by the AppDeploy platform; we do not store passwords.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We store only the transaction reference, the pack purchased, and the credits granted. We never see or store card numbers.

What we do not do

  • No advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with third parties beyond the processors named above.
  • No collection of data from children; this is a developer tool.

Deletion

To delete your stored reports and wallet record, contact us and we will remove them.

Changes

If our practices change, this page will be updated before the change takes effect.
