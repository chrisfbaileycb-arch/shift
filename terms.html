1. What SHIFT Pre-Flight is

SHIFT Pre-Flight is an automated, rule-based launch-readiness scanner for app developers. It analyzes configuration files and store-listing text that you provide and produces guidance-level findings about potential technical, policy, and marketing issues.

2. Not professional advice

The scanner's output is automated guidance only. It does not constitute certified legal advice, official security auditing, penetration testing, or regulatory compliance verification. Store policies, laws, and security threats change constantly; findings may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect (including false positives and false negatives).

You must independently test your software, verify current platform guidelines, and consult qualified legal counsel for binding regulatory compliance before relying on any finding.

3. No guarantee of store approval

A passing scan does not guarantee acceptance by Google Play, the Apple App Store, or any other distribution platform, and a flagged finding does not guarantee rejection. Store review decisions are made solely by the platforms.
