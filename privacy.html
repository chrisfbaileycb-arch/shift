Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version

SHIFT Pre-Flight runs entirely in your browser. The configuration files and marketing text you paste into the scanner are analyzed locally on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored by us.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No account is required and no personal information is requested.
  • Scan inputs (configs, descriptions) never leave your device.
  • We use no analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, or advertising SDKs.
  • Downloaded reports are generated locally and saved only where you choose.

Offline storage

If you install SHIFT Pre-Flight as an app, your browser caches the app's own static files (HTML, styles, icons) on your device so it works offline. This cache contains no user data and can be cleared at any time through your browser or device settings.

Third parties

The app makes no third-party network requests. If it is distributed through an app store (for example Google Play), the store itself may collect installation and crash statistics under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Children

SHIFT Pre-Flight is a developer tool, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If our data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date revised before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub repository: github.com/chrisfbaileycb-arch/shift.
